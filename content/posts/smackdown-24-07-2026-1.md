## Lainey Reid vs. Brie Bella:

Solid match. Lainey impressed me with how comfortable she is on the main roster in a singles match, but maybe one taunt to many? I loved the little taunts between spots but I think one or two less and it wouldn't have started to feel like she's doing it for the sake of doing them.

## Chelsea Green vs. Kiana James

Loved this match! Chelsea has always been entertaining in everything she's part of. I'm happy she gets to win a match outright instead of some distracting side business going on. The accidentally impressive moments are gold and she plays them off so well. I want to see more of this for her and let her start looking strong for bigger opportunities.
