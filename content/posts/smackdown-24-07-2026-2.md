This feels more like an obligatory promo than any real feud or build up to a big match, because honestly what's really new in this match up that we haven't seen from these two superstars?

Cody needs the title because without it, he doesn't really have a place in WWE. CM Punk is just giving us his greatest hits while retirement is creeping closer. 

All I can really say about this segment is... what's up with Cody's face? Either makeup screwed up or he got the wrong shade of self-tan because all I can think about while watching this segment is: "Is he slowly turning into Goldust from the inside out or does he maybe need to see a doctor?". 

But seriously, the segment feels like it was supposed to be two rivals building up to climactic match that is years in the making, but it feels flat in that regard. The history isn't really there, their paths weren't really building to a conflict, it just feels like epic conflict adjacent. It's there if you squint hard enough... 

The match at SummerSlam won't be bad, but I don't expect fireworks really. Who will win? Well that's the only redeeming thing about this match, I honestly have no idea which direction WWE will go with this.
