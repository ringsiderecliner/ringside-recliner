Sami Zayn has turned heel recently, but not in the traditional "Good guys finish last, so now I'm bad" kind of way. He framed it from the start that he's the real good guy here and everyone else are hypocrites. At first, yeah that's a solid way to get heat and it's annoying as fuck when he thinks he's owed something. Buuuutttt... is he starting to make sense? 

Here he has a backstage segment after the Nick v. Gunther segment outside the medical room. He hijacks Cathey Kelly's reporting to vent his frustrations and he starts making some good points. When he says there was a conspiracy to get the belt off him, I believe that. He was just crowned WWE champion and immediately was expected to defend it, where other champs could go weeks without defending it. Then he doesn't even defend it on SmackDown, he gets pulled into a RAW show to defend it, THEN his opponent gets taken out and instead of canceling the match they let someone who's been on vacation for months suddenly get a championship opportunity?? No way for Sami to prep for his opponent, no real rest, throwing him off by making him wrestle on RAW... honestly you can't blame Sami for thinking there is a conspiracy going on there. 

The segment actually got me from: "Sami is a whining bitch" to "Sami is actually a victim, un-ironically". Why are we being fed the same people over and over again in these title matches? Why can't Sami be a champion? If WWE wants to pivot away from the usual suspects when it comes to the championship conversation, then they have to try and give Sami some momentum here with everything he's saying, maybe have another overlooked or up-and-coming wrestler slightly agree with him. Maybe get some half support here and there from others. Just a: "Look Sami, I'm not saying you're wrong..." from a wrestler that's already endeared to the fans would go a long way. 

This could be the start to a very strong revolution and cause an abrupt but natural change of the guard in the roster when we're talking about the top guys currently.
